# 0047: Web: Grilling in the chat view

## Goal
Grilling questions arrive as a Conversation the person answers in their own words, instead of a form.

## Scope
- An Inbox Item "Answer Grilling" opens that person's Grilling Conversation.
- The header shows the round (Business, UX or Technical) and its progress, for example "3 of 5 answered".
- Questions that someone else already answered are shown collapsed, with who answered them.
- When the Brief Proposal is drafted, the starter's Conversation shows it as an Action Card.

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] Browser-verified with `VORASAAN_AI=fake`, using two users: a PO answers in chat and gets a follow-up. The Designer's round opens next. The starter approves the Brief Proposal from the card.

Tested: `apps/web/e2e/grilling.e2e.ts` (replaces the form-only flow): Pim starts a Grilling and opens her Grilling Conversation from the Inbox Item; the round header; Mint opens hers from a starter card and answers question 1; Pim is told with a quiet note and sees it collapsed "Answered by Mint", expanded on a click; a thin answer gets "Follow-up 1 of 2"; "Skip this question"; Mint answers the skipped one, which ends the round and opens the UX round in Pim's header; the same Conversation in Thai; Fern (Designer) answers the UX round on a 390px phone; Oak answers the technical round on the form; Pim approves the Brief Proposal from its card; then the Feature Suggestion → Spec flow as before. `apps/server/src/grilling/grilling-chat.api.test.ts` (real Postgres + `FakeAiPort`): Grilling Notes on every server-written message, the rounds and others' answers in the progress, the Brief Proposal card in the starter's Grilling Conversation (events, approve, only the owner), in a new Project Conversation when the starter has none (approved elsewhere → STALE), and on a Grilling drafted at start. `packages/shared/src/grilling-notes.test.ts`: the English and Thai texts.

## Decisions
- **Grilling Notes** (migration `20261001200000_grilling_notes`): every message the server writes in a Grilling Conversation is stored with `ConversationMessage.grillingNotes`, the facts it states (`GrillingNote` in `@vorasaan/shared`: INTRO, ASK, FOLLOW_UP, RECORDED, SKIPPED, ANSWERED_ELSEWHERE, WAITING, CLOSED, BRIEF_DRAFTED). `content` stays the same English text 0044 wrote (rendered from the notes), so the CLI, summaries and the 0044 tests read what they read before. `ConversationMessageView.grillingNotes` carries them; null on everything else, including messages written before this migration (the web then shows `content`).
- **Language:** the copy table for the server's fixed texts (`grillingNoteText(note, 'en' | 'th')`) lives in `@vorasaan/shared`. The server always stores English; the web renders the notes in the reader's language from the browser's `Accept-Language`, as 0046 did for the greeting (`lib/lang.ts`, moved out of the new-Conversation page). Rendering per reader instead of choosing a language when writing matters because most of these texts are written for someone who isn't making the request (a round opening, another holder's answer), so there is no request language to use. The chat's own Grilling copy (header, chip, placeholder, context line) is in `components/chat/grilling-copy.ts`. The AI follow-ups are unchanged: the AI writes them in the person's language. Round and Team Role names stay in English in both languages.
- **Notices versus the AI's voice:** ANSWERED_ELSEWHERE, WAITING, CLOSED and BRIEF_DRAFTED are notices (`isGrillingNotice`) and show as quiet centred notes (`role="note"`), without the spark. Questions, acknowledgements and follow-ups show as the AI. A `conversation.appended` message is usually a notice followed by the next question, so it is drawn part by part rather than as a whole; the next spoken part after a notice carries the spark again. A follow-up is labelled "Follow-up 1 of 2", as in the mockup.
- **Header:** `GrillingConversationProgress.rounds` lists the three rounds in order, each UPCOMING (not reached yet), ASKING, ANSWERED or SKIPPED with "answered of total". The highlighted round (`aria-current="step"`) is the one the Grilling is asking now, which after the person's own round is over is the next one, so a PO sees the Designer's round open. It sits at the top of the thread, as in the mockup, and scrolls with it.
- **Answered by others:** `GrillingConversationQuestion` gained `answer` and `followUps` (only once answered; everyone in the Organization already reads them on the Grilling page). Questions answered by someone other than the viewer are listed collapsed (`<details>`) under the round's introduction, with who answered and when; a click shows the answer and its follow-ups. The notice saying who answered stays in the thread where it happened.
- **Live:** a turn's answer and every `conversation.appended` event refresh the route, so the header and the answered list follow the Grilling.
- **Skip:** "Skip this question" (Thai "ข้ามคำถามนี้") is a chip above the composer while a question is being asked; it sends the skip word ("skip", or "ข้าม" in Thai) at once rather than filling the composer. During follow-ups the server treats it as 0044 does: the answer is recorded as it is. The old "skip" suggestion chip is gone.
- **Opening a Grilling Conversation:** the "Answer Grilling" Inbox Item and the starter card go to `/conversations/:id`, or, when the person has none yet, to `/grillings/:id/conversation`, a page that calls `POST /grillings/:id/conversation` (idempotent) and redirects; a 403 or 409 is shown there with a link to the Project's Grilling. Those links don't prefetch.
- **The Brief Proposal card:** 0044 created none. Now, in the transaction that drafts the Brief Proposal (the answer that ends the last round, a skipped round, or a start where every round is skipped), the server writes a BRIEF_DRAFTED message in the starter's most recently active Grilling Conversation of that Grilling, or, when they have none (an Admin outside the Project's Teams), in a new Project Conversation of theirs titled "Brief Proposal for <Project>", and a BRIEF_PROPOSAL Action Card under it, titled like a card from the `draftBriefProposal` tool. No tool call made it, so `toolCallId` is `grilling:<grillingId>`. After commit the starter gets `conversation.appended` and `conversation.actionCard.created`; the card's detail comes from `briefProposalDetail`, now shared with `ActionCardsService`. Approving it calls the same `BriefService.approve`; approving from the Inbox or the Grilling page makes it STALE, as for any card. Writing it in the drafting transaction keeps it atomic with the proposal; the message's position is taken inside that transaction.
- **Not here:** a stored language preference; translating the rest of the chat; the "Approve Brief Proposal" Inbox Item still opens the Grilling page.
